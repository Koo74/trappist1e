## 🛰️ Features

### Core Analysis
- **Interactive NASA Exoplanet Map** — Click anywhere on the TRAPPIST-1e surface to generate a custom site analysis instantly.
- **7-Criteria Weighted Scoring Engine** — Illumination (20%), Ice Access (20%), Subsurface Access (20%), Slope Safety (15%), Temperature (10%), Regolith (10%), Comms (5%).
- **7 Candidate Sites** — Pre-loaded with real data for Equatorial Ocean Belt, Terminator Edge Alpha, Night-Side Ice Sheet, Day-Side Desert, and more.
- **Seasonal Illumination Model** — Accounts for stellar flare variability on the M8V red dwarf host star.

### Advanced Tooling
- **⚖️ Compare Mode** — Side-by-side comparison of up to 3 sites with a Plotly radar chart and automatic 🏆 winner selection.
- **🛰️ Real-Time JWST Tracker** — Projected distance from the James Webb Space Telescope to your site.
- **🌪️ Flare Storm Calendar** — Full 12-month chart showing HIGH (red) vs LOW (green) stellar flare risk.
- **🕳️ Subsurface Ocean Map** — Probability of subsurface water access near your site.
- **⚡ Best Transit Window Recommender** — Calculates the optimal launch window (2050 → Fusion Drive).
- **🌡️ Day/Night Temperature Swing** — Plotly chart showing the 146.4-hour tidally locked cycle.
- **🧂 Toxic Atmosphere Warning** — Latitude-based atmospheric uncertainty levels with EVA suit recommendations.
- **🌅 Sun Position Animation** — Visualize sunlight changes across a full TRAPPIST-1e day.
- **🧬 Biosignature Probability Map** — JWST atmospheric data overlay.
- **🔬 JWST Atmospheric Data Feed** — Latest spectral features, CO2 levels, water vapor, and methane detections.
- **🌌 Night Sky from TRAPPIST-1e** — Constellations visible from your site (completely different from Earth).
- **💎 Mineral Resource Map** — Site-specific mineral composition with mining value scores.
- **🧪 Simulation Lab** — What-if analysis for crew size, habitat mass, and power source.
- **💾 Persistent Memory** — SQLite-backed analysis history that survives page refreshes.
- **🛰️ Live Solar System Tracker** — Embedded NASA Eyes on Exoplanets feed.
